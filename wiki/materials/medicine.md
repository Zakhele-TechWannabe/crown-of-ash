# Medicine and alchemy

Body Tempering's later gates need particular medicines, not a generic potion currency.

| Medicine | Gates using it |
|---|---|
| Iron-bark baths | Iron Bone (4) |
| Beast marrow broth | Jade Marrow (5), Living Blood (6) |
| Seven-leaf spirit herb | Five Viscera (7), Open Meridians (8), Perfected Body (9) |

Wild supply includes bark patches, spirit herbs and hunting products. Seven-leaf grows at the sect spring, where [entry law](../people/law.md) can make gathering dangerous. [Palace candidates](../people/fifty.md) receive gate-medicine stipends rather than competing in the village rice economy.

!!! admonition "Planned"
    A complete alchemy system would transform ingredients under controlled furnace conditions. Its named disciplines are currently catalogue entries without full detail. Pills, complete beast-essence purification and every proposed spiritual ingredient recipe should not be assumed playable.

This page will document medicine yields, processing and recipes when authoritative implementations establish them. See [Body Tempering](../cultivation/body-tempering.md), [materials](goods.md) and [beast cultivation](../wildlife/cultivation.md).
