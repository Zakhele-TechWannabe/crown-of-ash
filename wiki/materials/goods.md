# Goods and materials

The village gathers, produces, stores and consumes goods. Food supports life and work; materials support construction and crafts. A catalogue entry can describe a spirit material even when obtaining or using it is still limited.

Displayed material quantities use the existing half-unit ledger convention: display quantities are **twice internal stocks**. Read each good's own unit instead of assuming all stock numbers are weights or rations.
