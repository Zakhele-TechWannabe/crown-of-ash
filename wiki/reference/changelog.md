# Wiki and mechanics changes

## 2026-11-22: combat phase 2

- **Weapons:** people fight with the weapon of their best weapon skill, or bare hands. Ten weapons with power, reach, tempo and parry; hunters take a spear.
- **Reach:** long weapons hold short ones off; the shorter side must step inside, and the longer side steps back out.
- **Parries, ripostes, feints and shields** for skilled fighters. Details on [fight resolution](../combat/resolution.md).

## 2026-11-22

Initial player wiki created from the current design notes, configuration and certification code.

- **Layer 5:** current certification uses **35 QU/hour for the best core hour on 30 valid days**, with **400 QU** capacity. The 21 November vessel decision replaces the older strict 90-QU/hour wall; 40+ remains a distinction.
- **Layer 7:** the weekly drill now pumps both return lanes with guided Circulate. Its rate uses actual simultaneous running time, and days without the drill retain the last branch rate. Standard: **25 QU/hour**, error **≤0.15**.
- **Layer 9:** the second full-vessel milestone moves to Foundation preparation. Earlier functions can be shown within seven days instead of all on one day. The daily core standard is **35 QU/hour**; **75** is distinguished and **150** legendary.
- **Programme choice:** remembers peak felt strain, judges candidates against both trial and memory, and habituates injury-free strain up to **0.15**. From layer 8, filtering stays at least **0.25**.
- **Latest prepared Perfect fixture:** cautious cultivator reaches layer 9 on **day 1015**; the other on **day 2442**. These supersede earlier fixture dates and blocked results, without promising ordinary-world timing.

See the complete [layer table](../cultivation/qi-condensation.md) and [programme explanation](../cultivation/programmes.md). Future entries should distinguish mechanic changes from documentation-only corrections.
