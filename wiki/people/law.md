# Law and crime

The sect grounds are restricted. A servant may enter their workplace during working hours with a work pass. A pass does not permit unrelated places or a night visit. The outer village and roads are outside the guarded grounds.

Trespass triggers pursuit only when seen. Buildings, walls, trees and terrain can block sight; darkness reduces it. Stealth and hunting knowledge can lower detection risk. Escaping a seen breach puts a person on the hunt list, and a guardian may later pursue them into the village.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---:|
| Day sight cap | 60 tiles |
| Night sight cap | 6 tiles |
| Base detection factor, day / night | 0.90 / 0.25 |
| Hunt priority half-life | 30 days |
| Trail goes cold | 21 days |
| Minimum body layer for adult kin joining a defence | 3 |

Repeated offences raise priority, but a trail can go cold before its mathematical priority fades away. A pursuit depends on guardian energy and the partner remaining home. Such fights can kill.
