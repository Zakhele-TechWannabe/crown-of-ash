# Villagers, families and the heart

Villagers are individuals with households, kin, skills, attributes, traits and memories. Families connect generations; marriage, care, grief and grudges influence later choices. A shared household can matter in a guardian pursuit: adult kin with sufficient Body Tempering may stand together.

Relationships track more than liking. Shared work and meals build familiarity; teaching and help can build trust; theft and betrayal can damage it. Traits affect compatibility. Two curious people may find common ground, while diligence and laziness can clash.

The mind weighs urges such as fear, ambition, devotion, pride, curiosity and restlessness. Emotions and lasting experiences change how those choices feel. A cautious person may avoid a dangerous trip; a hungry or ambitious person may take it. Heaven does not choose every action for them.
