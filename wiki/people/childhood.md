# Childhood

Children do no productive work and gain no skill XP before twelve. Watching a trade builds interest rather than experience.

| Age | Life stage | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Infant | At home, without a map walker or duties; parental care reduces available work |
| 3–5 | Little one | Plays near home; family contact and upbringing shape the heart |
| 6–11 | Child | Playmates, rivalries, tagging along, talent stories and occasional wandering |
| 12 | Trade choice | Interest, aptitude, village need and family pull influence the chosen job |

A parent's work reduction starts at **40%** in the first year and **20%** in the second. Warmth, hunger, parental death and fighting can leave lasting emotional effects. Childhood hunger can reduce potential after **30 hungry days**, by **1** per interval, up to **3** total.

A talent story reflects real attributes, potential or comprehension; it is not a gift of XP. Wandering can cause scrapes of **2–5 days**, and wildlife encounters can be dangerous.
