# The Fifty and Palace disciples

Heaven begins with fifty twelve-year-old candidates in the hidden Heavenly Dao Palace. They are people in the same population as villagers, with different talents and hearts. They receive a Perfect-capable tempering method, teaching and basic gate medicine, and are provisioned outside the village rice economy.

| Palace rule | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total places | 50 |
| Apostle seats | 5 |
| Tournament interval | 5 years |
| Resource winners | Top 10 |
| Top-tier training multiplier | 1.3 |
| Basic / top medicine doses | 1 / 2 |
| Annual heaven-stone income | 1 |
| Annual Heaven's Will income | 6 |
| Apostle restoration time | 10 years |
| Restoration cost | 10 heaven-stone + 30 Heaven's Will |

Candidates compete in a single-elimination tournament. Wounds still need healing even though bouts stop below lethal harm. Completing Body Tempering with every grade Perfect can earn an Apostle seat; a Perfect candidate can challenge when seats are occupied. Other graduates become envoys.

Envoy death is final. Apostles enter restoration, which waits if resources are insufficient. Restoration preserves identity, grades, memories and ties; it does not age them during the restoration period.

Recruitment can involve an invitation or a call to Heaven. People can refuse; pilgrims must travel and pass admission tests. Tests weigh talent, body, will and character, with admission ages **10–30**. A call has a **365-day** cooldown.
