# The living ecosystem

Wildlife exists at two scales. Monthly species cohorts represent many animals, while daily persistent records represent notable predators, awakened beasts and animals involved in stories. Named animals are not simply interchangeable encounter spawns.

Food, season, crowding, predation, births, ageing, starvation and dispersal change populations. Zero-start species remain absent; no guaranteed awakening or disaster is manufactured to fill a story slot. Hunting removes actual animals, and pen raids affect wildlife livestock inventory and food stock.

Encounters focus on people who enter relevant places: hunters, foragers, woodcutters and other workers, plus wandering children. Quiet meetings usually flee; wounded, starving or protective beasts may fight. Outcomes can include villager death.

!!! note "Unverified"
    The current ecology is provisional. Ground-sampled habitat, actual qi-site geography, wolf-pack aggregation, detailed territory and energy budgets, and exact route-distance checks remain incomplete. Wildlife livestock is not yet a shared production model with the village herd.
