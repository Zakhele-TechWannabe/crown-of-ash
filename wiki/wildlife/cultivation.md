# Beast cultivation

Awakening is rare. Eligible animals can develop nine bodily layers equivalent to Body Tempering, rather than using the human Qi Condensation circuit. Species affinity, site exposure and exceptional events affect awakening.

| Exposure | Base awakening hazard per animal-year |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary land | 0.00001 |
| Weak qi site | 0.0001 |
| Strong qi site | 0.001 |

Affinity modifies these hazards, with an overall cap of **0.005**. Exceptional events have a separate **0.005** chance and a **365-day** refractory interval. These are simulation probabilities, not guaranteed waiting times.

| Completed layer pursued | Minimum years | Lifespan multiplier after completion |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 1.2 |
| 2 | 1 | 1.45 |
| 3 | 2 | 1.8 |
| 4 | 3 | 2.3 |
| 5 | 4 | 3 |
| 6 | 5 | 4 |
| 7 | 6 | 5 |
| 8 | 8 | 6.5 |
| 9 | 10 | 8 |

Completed layers slow future biological ageing; they do not rejuvenate chronological age. Methods and catalysts can arise through time at a claimed site and rare learning or forage opportunities. Fallen cores can feed some carnivores and omnivores.

## Spirit and demonic paths

Purity, resentment and instability matter over time. Ordinary predation or one kill does not automatically make a beast demonic. The demonic conditions require sustained low purity and pressure; recovery also requires sustained improvement.

| Path condition | Threshold and duration |
|---|---|
| Spirit | Purity ≥0.60 for 3 months |
| Demonic | Purity <0.30 and max(resentment, instability) >0.60 for 3 months |
| Recovery | Purity ≥0.70 and max(resentment, instability) <0.30 for 6 months |

!!! note "Unverified"
    Site locations and resource competition remain provisional. Complete essence purification, core assimilation limits, anatomy-specific products and nonlethal harvesting remain incomplete. Do not treat every proposed beast product as an available medicine recipe.
