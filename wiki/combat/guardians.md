# Stone guardians

Stone swordsmen enforce the sect's entry law. Pairs stand on plinths at gates, and one partner stays home when the other pursues. They are stone enforcers, not people with ordinary kin grudges.

| Guardian property | Value |
|---|---:|
| Power / guard | 66 / 70 |
| Speed / breath | 9 / 30 |
| Will | 9 (never yields) |
| Maximum energy | 100 |
| Plinth recharge | 10/day |
| Walking cost inside / outside grounds | 0.5 / 1.5 per tile |
| Fight energy cost | 10 |
| Return reserve | 25 |
| Mending on plinth | 2 harm/day |
| Refuses new pursuit above harm | 60 |
| Breaks at accumulated harm | 100 |

Harm persists between fights. Cracks appear at **30** and **60** harm. Mending works only on the plinth; rubble never mends. A broken swordsman is permanently lost. A guardian that reaches zero energy freezes where it stands.

Detection depends on visibility, cover and time of day. Escaping after being seen can still lead to a later village pursuit. Adult kin may defend a hunted household together. The guardian card shows energy, harm, state and fight history; rubble records who broke it and when.

!!! admonition "Planned"
    A future sect may support guardians and formations may improve recharge or mending. Frozen recovery and replacement should not be assumed available.
