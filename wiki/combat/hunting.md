# Beasts and hunting

Hunters draw real deer and boar from populations around the Eastern Hunting Grounds. Hunting removes animals; breeding and ecology govern recovery. The hunt is not an unlimited abstract supply roll.

Finding prey, ambushing it and surviving a fight are different questions. Strength, skill, tempering and available qi affect the fight. A wounded or cornered animal, a mother protecting young, or a starving predator can be much more dangerous than a quiet encounter.
