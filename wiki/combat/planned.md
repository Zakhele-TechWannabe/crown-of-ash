# Planned combat and qi phenomena

!!! admonition "Planned"
    These features are designed or proposed, but the combat implementation notes do not report them as built. They are not current layer rewards.

| Feature | Intended role |
|---|---|
| Foundation flying swords | Spiritual sense supports sustained flying weapons; distinct from the layer-8 sword pulse |
| Full martial programmes | Authored instruction sequences and a complete equipment system beyond current skill-selected weapons |
| Elemental qi arts | Paid elemental packets and effects through the same bodily routes |
| Local qi phenomena | Mist, gusts, frost or heat shimmer around the cultivator, paid from reserve |
| Formation barriers and killing arrays | External qi networks supplied by veins or stored resources |
| Heavenly phenomena | Larger breakthrough signs and tribulation associated with Heaven and higher realms |

A local qi phenomenon does not give a cultivator control over valley weather. Formation proposals include interception and storage, but their proposed costs are not listed as current combat prices here.

This page will track these features as implementation evidence becomes available. Current abilities are on [qi in combat](qi.md); next-realm progression is on [Foundation Establishment](../cultivation/foundation-establishment.md).

!!! note "Unverified"
    The design's “phase 2 not yet built” list is stale for weapon reach, tempo, parries, ripostes, feints and shields. Those are present in the current code; see [fight resolution](resolution.md). This does not establish the full proposed martial-programme engine.
