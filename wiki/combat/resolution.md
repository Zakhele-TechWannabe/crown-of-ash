# How fights resolve

Fights run blow by blow. Each fighter has power, guard, speed, breath and will. Faster fighters strike more often; heavy blows stagger; winded fighters become slower and weaker. Motive affects when a fighter yields or runs, so a parent defending young can behave differently from a fearful animal.

People's attributes, martial skills, tempering, age and injuries shape their physical profile. People and beasts use the same resolution system. Qi assists only when a real reserve can pay through a usable route.

| Accumulated harm | Wound |
|---:|---|
| 0–<15 | Unhurt |
| 15–<35 | Bruised |
| 35–<60 | Hurt |
| 60–<100 | Badly hurt |
| ≥100 | Dying |

| Fight rule | Value |
|---|---:|
| Base strike interval | 12 seconds ÷ speed |

A spar or tournament can stop earlier than a lethal fight. Injuries still affect subsequent activity. Reaching dying can kill; it is not an ordinary recovery tier.

A large [qi reserve](../cultivation/vessel.md) does not instantly pass through a narrow wrist. See [qi abilities](qi.md), [hunting](hunting.md) and [guardians](guardians.md).

## Weapons and technique

The current fighter selects the weapon matching the person's highest positive weapon skill, otherwise bare hands. This is a skill-based choice, rather than a verified inventory/equipment system. Longer reach can hold a shorter fighter off until they step inside; long weapons lose power when crowded. Tempo changes time between blows. Skill can enable parries, ripostes and feints. A compatible free hand plus shield skill provides a shield guard bonus.

| Weapon | Power multiplier | Reach (metres) | Tempo multiplier | Parry factor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Unarmed | 1.0 | 0.7 | 0.85 | 0.5 |
| Staff | 1.15 | 2.0 | 1.0 | 1.2 |
| Spear | 1.3 | 2.4 | 1.1 | 0.8 |
| Blade | 1.35 | 1.0 | 0.95 | 1.0 |
| Straight sword | 1.3 | 1.1 | 0.9 | 1.2 |
| Axe | 1.45 | 0.9 | 1.2 | 0.6 |
| Hammer | 1.55 | 0.9 | 1.3 | 0.5 |
| Polearm | 1.45 | 2.6 | 1.25 | 0.8 |
| Twin weapons | 1.2 | 0.9 | 0.75 | 1.0 |
| Chain weapons | 1.2 | 2.0 | 1.1 | 0.4 |

A smaller tempo multiplier means shorter intervals: twin weapons strike about 1.7 times as often as a hammer. Hunters always take a spear. Beasts and stone swordsmen fight with natural weapons (no power multiplier, their own reach, 1 m by default).

### Reach

When the two reaches differ by **0.5 m** or more, the fight opens at the longer weapon's distance, where it strikes with **+10% power per metre** of advantage. The shorter fighter spends its turns stepping inside: a **35%** base chance, **±30%** per unit of speed ratio, **+1%** per footwork level, kept between **15%** and **85%**. A failed step is "held off" and wastes the turn. Once inside, weapons of **2 m** or longer are crowded (**×0.8** power), and the longer fighter tries to step back out on its own turns with the same odds. A stagger it lands also pushes the other back out.

### Parry, riposte and feint

- **Parry:** a miss against a defender with weapon skill can be parried, with chance **0.5 × parry factor × skill ÷ (skill + 10)**. The parrier answers within **0.4 s** with a riposte at **×1.15** power.
- **Feint:** an attacker with weapon skill **5+**, facing a guard at least **80%** of their own power, feints with chance **skill ÷ (skill + 20)**. That blow meets the guard at **×0.75**, costs **0.3 s**, and cannot be parried.
- **Shield:** shield skill with bare hands or a one-handed weapon (dao, jian, axe, hammer) multiplies guard by **1.25**.

### Measured

1,000 seeded fights between fully body-tempered fighters: a spear beats bare hands **88%**; twin weapons beat a hammer **67%**; a skill-25 jian beats an unskilled jian **55%** with about two parries a fight; a shield and dao beat a dao **72%**. An untempered villager does about twice the harm to a tiger with a spear (3.5) as bare-handed (1.8), though the tiger still wins.
