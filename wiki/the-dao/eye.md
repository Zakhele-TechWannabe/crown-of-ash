# The Dao's Eye

Press **E** to open the Eye, or use the top bar. **L** opens the valley book; opening either book closes the other. The Eye is an inspection and finding tool rather than a source of free progress.

| Finder category | What it reads |
|---|---|
| People | Living people, place groupings, rankings, cultivation state and requests |
| Wildlife | Species and individual records |
| Medicine | Wild patches and seven-leaf stocks |
| Materials | Stores and goods |
| Places | Map features, destinations, settlements and local qi descriptions |

Search includes people and dead names. Selecting someone gives a summary and **Open their book**. Strength sorting uses certified meridian layer, then body layer, then reserve. Contribution ranking is unavailable because the simulation does not record an authoritative contribution score.

“Village” in the finder uses current position inside the village footprint and its apron, rather than only home assignment. Palace membership and sect faction membership have their own grouping rules.

The World ledger links recorded deaths, causes, elemental shares, veins and first breakthrough milestones. Qi flow amounts are **today's figures**, because the field retains a daily ledger rather than historical flow sums. Deaths and milestones can include archived events beyond the recent chronicle.
