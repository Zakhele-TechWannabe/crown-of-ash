# Playing the Heavenly Dao

You watch a world whose people act for themselves. Heaven's own long project is [the Fifty](../people/fifty.md): raise candidates, follow their distinct progress, recruit when places open, and maintain the resources needed by Apostles.

The world's qi obeys sources, paths, pressure, cost and control. A manual supplies instructions; it does not silently open channels or guarantee success. Environmental advantage changes opportunities, while bodily foundations and decisions still matter.

Use [the Dao's Eye](eye.md) to find people, wildlife, medicine, material stores and places. The valley book presents ongoing cultivation and society records. [Qi decrees](decrees.md) already have simulation rules, while their Eye mutation workflow remains future work.

!!! admonition "Planned"
    Envoy mission orders, directly playing an Apostle, the proposed Dao's Forge for composing manuals, and broader intervention workflows are later features. Their design descriptions do not establish present buttons or guaranteed outcomes.

Start with [the valley](../world/valley.md) or examine [cultivation](../cultivation/overview.md).
