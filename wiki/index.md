# Crown of Ash Wiki

Crown of Ash (working title) is a xianxia valley simulation. You are the Heavenly Dao, watching an autonomous world, a village and a sect, while raising Heaven's own cultivators. People have families, trades, ambitions and dangers of their own.

Start with [the valley](world/valley.md), [your role](the-dao/role.md), or [cultivation](cultivation/overview.md). Open the in-game valley book with **L**, or [the Dao's Eye](the-dao/eye.md) with **E** to find people and places.

## Reading this wiki

Pages describe the current implementation unless a **Planned** box says otherwise. **Unverified** boxes identify conflicting sources, provisional models or misleading readouts. Fixture results are measured examples from controlled runs, not promises about every new world. The reference date is **22 November 2026**.
