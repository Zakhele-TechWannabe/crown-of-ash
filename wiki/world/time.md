# Time, seasons and saves

The world advances on dated simulation steps. Cultivation and environmental qi settle across daily boundaries; wildlife population changes include monthly updates. Fast-forward is intended to preserve the same outcomes as daily play.

Spring, summer, autumn and winter affect supplies. Autumn wood gathering increases, winter household fuel use doubles, and deep-winter quarry and mine shifts become smithy maintenance. Goods record their gathering or harvest seasons. Wildlife's seasonal food multipliers are:

| Spring | Summer | Autumn | Winter |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.15 | 1.30 | 1.10 | 0.65 |

There is one normal world save slot. Time does not advance while the application is closed. The game saves at crossed day boundaries, periodically (including while paused), and on orderly close. A previous-save backup provides a fallback. If both saves cannot load, play pauses and preserves them until you explicitly choose a new world.

Use the confirmed **New world** action only when you intend to replace the current valley. See [death and lifespan](../people/death.md) for why years matter.
