The sect's Spirit Spring, peak, halls, courts and caves lie within guarded grounds. The Servants' Village, outer fields, West Road and Hunters' Lodge are outside those grounds. A work pass permits only the relevant workplace and working hours: see [law and crime](../people/law.md).

The Palace is home to [the Fifty](../people/fifty.md). The older name “Heaven's Terrace” refers to this Heaven-owned training settlement.
