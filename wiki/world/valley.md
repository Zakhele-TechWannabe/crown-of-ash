# The valley and map

The world contains a Servants' Village below the sect grounds, roads to the Western Market City, eastern hunting country and the hidden Heavenly Dao Palace on Heavenly Ridge. The valley is more than its visible walkers: population positions and simulation records determine where a person actually is.

The atlas covers **360 × 240 cells**. Each atlas cell spans **16 × 16 ground tiles**. The atlas offers a qi density overlay and element selector; use these with [qi and veins](qi.md) to understand why one meditation spot differs from another.

The [valley book](../the-dao/eye.md) groups cultivation, Heaven, people, valley resources and wildlife. A person's book connects their life to their current cultivation state.

See [places](places.md) for destinations and [time and saves](time.md) for the continuing world.
