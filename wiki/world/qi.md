# Qi field and spirit veins

Ambient qi is a local resource with a finite supply. Density is concentration, not daily production. A rich location can be drained; a mountain is not automatically qi-rich.

Five elemental inventories are conserved: **Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth**. Purity, stability, corruption and yin–yang polarity describe quality. Yin does not mean evil. The ten spirit-root affinities use these five environmental elements, with derived preferences for Wind, Thunder, Ice, Light and Shadow.

## Veins

A spirit vein is a network of underground sources and channels. Sources have reserves and regeneration; channels have delivery limits and integrity. Upstream emission spends supply before downstream emission. Generated outlet qi is paid from source reserves, rather than granted freely.

| Grade | Name | Rated output (QU/day) | Full reserve (QU) | Outlet density bonus |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Faint | 20 | 7,300 | 1 |
| 2 | Lesser | 80 | 29,200 | 2 |
| 3 | Established | 320 | 116,800 | 4 |
| 4 | Superior | 1,280 | 467,200 | 8 |
| 5 | Great | 5,120 | 1,868,800 | 16 |
| 6 | Peerless | 20,480 | 7,475,200 | 64 |

The generated world adds **48 wilderness veins**, excluding grade 6. The Palace and sect spring have configured sources; their surroundings do not all share a single density. The Heavenly Origin is indestructible. [Sealing](../the-dao/decrees.md) stops new emission rather than erasing ambient qi already present.

## Reading density

| Density (QU per standard volume) | Description |
|---:|---|
| <0.25 | Spiritually barren |
| 0.25–<1 | Thin qi |
| 1–<2 | Ordinary ambient qi |
| 2–<8 | Qi-rich |
| 8–<32 | Abundant spiritual qi |
| 32–<128 | A spiritual sanctuary |
| ≥128 | Immortal-estate concentration |

A cell represents **2,000 standard volumes**. Its mass is density × 2,000 QU. Consumers share actual local supply, so a good [programme](../cultivation/programmes.md) still needs a suitable location.
