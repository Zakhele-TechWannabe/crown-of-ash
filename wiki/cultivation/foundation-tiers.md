# Foundation quality and vessel tiers

Body Tempering grades and vessel tiers describe different achievements. A Perfect tempering grade is not automatically a heavenly-gifted vessel.

| Vessel tier | Safe-pressure boundary used to classify held full pressure |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary | Below 1.333 |
| Genius | At least 1.333, below 1.5 |
| Heavenly-gifted | At least 1.5 |

The game records the greatest pressure actually held while full. Classification compares that held pressure with each tier boundary multiplied by **0.975**, the full-vessel fraction. A theoretical safe-pressure potential that was never held is not the same evidence.

!!! note "Unverified"
    Design prose calls the genius boundary **1.33**; current certification code uses **1.333**. This page follows the code. These vessel distinctions do not mean Foundation Establishment has been implemented.

The layer-3 standard is attainable at an ordinary body's personal full pressure. The former requirement to hold pressure 1.5 would have excluded ordinary vessels. See [the dantian](vessel.md), [walls](walls.md) and [Foundation Establishment](foundation-establishment.md).
