# Programme choice and danger sense

Cultivators choose how they practise. Fear, ambition, pride, curiosity and restlessness affect initial filtering, experimentation and acceptable strain. A strong method is still evaluated on the cultivator's own body and current source.

The starting programme is held for **90 days**, with later reviews on the same personal schedule. Candidates include the current programme, standard alternatives and changes to filtering. A copied-body trial estimates work, residue and strain; a candidate causing injury or exceeding accepted strain is rejected.

## Learning from a whole stint

A single safe-looking day can hide strain that builds over weeks. Programme choice now remembers the peak **felt strain** built by the outgoing programme, and judges a returning candidate against the worse of that memory and its one-day trial. This prevents repeated switching between a light filter that gradually becomes uncomfortable and a heavy filter that drains the vessel.

**Habituation** raises accepted strain when a completed stint caused no injury. This learned contribution is capped at **0.15**. It is not immunity to deviation, nor a replacement for all personality-based risk tolerance.

From certified **layer 8**, filtering has a minimum setting of **0.25**. Layer 9 needs demonstrated mixed-qi processing; work-only scoring had previously selected zero filtering and blocked that proof.

Danger sense includes channel strain as well as dantian strain. Dangerous cultivation can injure, scar or kill; travellers draw no new ambient qi before an authoritative arrival but still face stored-qi upkeep and exposure.
