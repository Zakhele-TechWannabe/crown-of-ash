## Conditions behind the table

Valid practice requires completed tempering and a revealed root, stable execution, no new injury or death, strain no greater than **0.20**, sound anatomy (integrity at least **0.85**) and no ongoing society injury recovery. Relevant timed evidence requires sessions of at least **2 hours**. Later certification attempts use a personal weekly slot; satisfying a record quota does not guarantee today's physical trial will pass.

Layer 2 measures the minimum flow along a complete core circuit, choosing between its abdominal and lumbar branches. Its return-day counter resets on invalid execution; a valid low-output day does not itself reset it. Layer 5 measures the best single hour, while layer 9 measures the daily session average. Layer 7 divides branch flow by the time both lanes actually run, not the full four-hour session.

Layer 3 uses funded reserve integration as its controlled draw. Fill must cover a positive draw; this is not a separate combat discharge test. [Filling policy](vessel.md) helps hold the vessel still for that milestone.

At layer 9, controlled draw, a qualifying output hour, mixed-qi processing and a successful release must each have been shown within the current seven-day window (the code uses age **<7 days**). All four limb records and the earlier quotas must remain satisfied. These functions do not all need to occur on the same day; release is tested weekly.

## Reading the fixture dates

The dates are absolute simulated days from the final **2026-11-22 “Programme choice remembers strain”** thirty-year Perfect fixture. Both people have a prepared Perfect foundation. They are controlled examples, not a timetable for an ordinary new villager. Earlier sections of the same notes report blocked fixtures and earlier dates; those are historical.
