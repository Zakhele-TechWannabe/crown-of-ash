# Body Tempering

Tempering builds the body that later carries qi. Each completed layer keeps a **Low, Middle, High or Perfect** grade. Method quality, teachers, gifts, patience and polishing can influence the result; a method's grade cap matters.

| Layer | Name | Minimum method | Gate medicine |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Copper Skin | plain exercise | — |
| 2 | Dense Flesh | plain exercise | — |
| 3 | Steel Sinew | folk drills | — |
| 4 | Iron Bone | a tempering manual | iron-bark baths |
| 5 | Jade Marrow | a tempering manual | beast marrow broth |
| 6 | Living Blood | a tempering manual | beast marrow broth |
| 7 | Five Viscera | a complete method | a seven-leaf spirit herb |
| 8 | Open Meridians | a complete method | a seven-leaf spirit herb |
| 9 | Perfected Body | a complete method | a seven-leaf spirit herb |

Labour can contribute to the first two layers. Dedicated regimens allow up to **4 hours/day**. A suitable teacher requires a **2-layer** lead and improves training rate by **1.5×**. Pushing practice adds strain; rest and recovery matter.

A complete Perfect body is valuable even without qi: [combat](../combat/qi.md) still depends on the body's strength, skill and tissue tolerance. Completed grades also affect different [meridians](meridians.md) by different weights, so one average grade cannot explain every channel.

[Medicine](../materials/medicine.md) supplies later gates. [The Fifty](../people/fifty.md) receive better access, but individuals still make different choices.
