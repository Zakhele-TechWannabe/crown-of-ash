# Foundation Establishment

!!! admonition "Planned"
    Foundation Establishment is the next fundamental transformation after integrated Qi Condensation. The current physical progression does not implement its breakthrough walls or automatic advancement. Named higher realms and lifespan values in configuration are not a playable path by themselves.

The vessel already switches to fill-first preparation when pursuing the next milestone after layer 9. That prepares a full vessel; it does not award the realm.

Foundation's combat design reserves **spiritual sense and flying swords** for this realm. Qi Condensation's layer-8 tethered sword pulse is a smaller, distinct ability.

This page will cover Foundation's entry conditions, transformation, failures, stages and new abilities when those are implemented. For now, see [Qi Condensation](qi-condensation.md), [vessel filling](vessel.md) and [planned combat](../combat/planned.md).
