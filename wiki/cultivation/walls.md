# Walls and distinctions

A wall is a physical or control limit, rather than a failed random breakthrough roll. Check the person's missing evidence: is the reserve too small, a return route stalled, filtering absent, a branch uncoordinated or a release too weak?

| Wall | Current standard | Stronger distinction or historical target |
|---|---|---|
| Full vessel | 97.5% of personal safe pressure | Genius / heavenly-gifted held pressure |
| Layer 5 output | Best hour ≥35 QU/hour | 40+ distinguished; historical strict 90 |
| Layer 7 control | Branches ≥25 QU/hour; error ≤0.15 | Error ≤0.08 |
| Layer 8 release | ≥30% of a 0.10-QU packet at one tile | ≥70% |
| Layer 9 integration | Daily core ≥35 QU/hour | 75 distinguished; 150 legendary |

Increasing channel width alone does not fix every wall. The November fixture reached about **39 QU/hour** even after roughly doubled adaptation: drive rather than width was limiting. Filling can also reduce returning output because the vessel pushes back.

The later fixes changed the flow or evidence rules: guided branch drills for layer 7, Foundation rather than layer-9 filling, a seven-day integrated evidence window, and [programme memory](programmes.md). The [latest dates](qi-condensation.md) show both prepared Perfect fixture people reaching layer 9.

!!! note "Unverified"
    Older dated reports say natural layers 2–9 were unreachable. They describe earlier implementations and are superseded for the final prepared fixture. They do not establish how quickly an ordinary unprepared population will qualify or advance now.
