# The dantian and vessel

The lower dantian stores refined qi. **Capacity** is storage size; **safe pressure** is the body's tolerance for filling it. Reserve is spendable energy, while paid opening and enlargement work represent permanent development.

## Capacity ceiling

The initial capacity is **100 QU**. The enlargement ceiling is:

**initial capacity × dantian safe pressure × 1.5^(layer pursued) × 10^(realm − 1)**

Here the realm factor starts at **1 for Qi Condensation**. The current function caps the pursued-layer exponent at nine. An ordinary safe-pressure-1 vessel therefore has a peak Qi Condensation ceiling of about **3,844 QU**. This is a ceiling, not qi granted instantly. Work refused by the storage ceiling can develop channels instead.

## Full means personal full

A full vessel holds **97.5% of its own safe pressure**, rather than requiring everyone to reach pressure 1.5. Safe pressure and capacity differ between bodies. [Vessel tiers](foundation-tiers.md) record what pressure was actually held.

Before pursuing **layer 3** and **Foundation (the next milestone after layer 9)**, the cultivator stops enlarging until the vessel fills and draws only overflow. Filling no longer happens before layer 9. A full dantian pushes back on returning flow, which can obstruct the circuit needed for integrated practice. It is also deliberately not a preparation rule for layer 5.

Ordinary reserve integration protects a **70% reserve floor**. Having residue occupy space does not make it spendable reserve. The special fill-first policy protects the higher milestone fill target.

!!! admonition "Planned"
    Filling before Foundation is an implemented preparation policy. The Foundation breakthrough itself is not implemented. Future treasures that alter the personal full-vessel limit are not an available progression route established by these notes.
