# Cultivation and realms

Body Tempering develops the material foundation. Qi Condensation opens and develops a real spiritual vessel and network. A large reserve, a high skill or an impressive manual is not itself a breakthrough.

| Realm | Current status |
|---|---|
| Mortal | Ordinary life before cultivation |
| Body Tempering | Nine bodily layers, each with a lasting grade |
| Qi Condensation | Nine certifications based on committed practice and trials |
| Foundation Establishment | Designed next transformation; physical breakthrough not implemented |
| Core Formation, Nascent Soul | Named in configuration; later progression |

Physical qi practice starts after all **nine Body Tempering layers** and an awakened/revealed spirit root. A new physical body begins with no stored qi or inherited certifications. Older abstract qi records do not become a free physical endowment.

Study [Body Tempering](body-tempering.md), then [layers 1–9](qi-condensation.md). The [vessel](vessel.md), [meridians](meridians.md) and [programme](programmes.md) explain why two people progress differently. [Walls and distinctions](walls.md) separates the current standard from exceptional performance.

!!! note "Unverified"
    Older designs used random breakthroughs, qi-bar costs and a thirteen-layer genius exception. Current code certifies nine Qi Condensation layers through physical evidence and disables the legacy random advancement path. [Foundation](foundation-establishment.md) does not yet follow automatically.
