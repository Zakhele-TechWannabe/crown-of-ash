# Village materials

Materials use the existing half-unit ledger convention: display quantities are twice internal stocks. Goods declare their own unit, grade, icon, colour, source, season, yields and recipes. Salt remains in Food / Preserved & pantry.

Eight adults remain field hands. Seven existing adults share morning food duties with afternoon material work; the remaining food specialists and two children retain their routines. Listed daily yields represent these small household-scale work sessions. Deep-winter quarry and mine shifts become smithy maintenance. Hemp is harvested on days 153–182; autumn wood gathering rises by 50%, and winter household fuel use doubles.

Material sites are foreground drawings, outside the chunk rasterizer. Footprints are selected against dry terrain, existing surfaces, building art and food footprints, with slope and workplace-distance checks.
